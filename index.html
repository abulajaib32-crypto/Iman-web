About the Frontend Track

The Frontend Track teaches the skills behind modern, interactive websites through practice and projects.

What the track is

A beginner-friendly part of IMAN Elevate Bootcamp focused on building websites.

What students learn

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, responsive design, and how to make pages interactive.

Who it is for

Students and beginners who want to learn frontend development step by step.

What you can achieve

Build and share your own websites and get a base for further learning.

What you'll learn

Six main topics, taught in this order.

1

HTML

Structure webpages with headings, text, links, images, and forms.

2

CSS

Style pages and build clear, attractive layouts.

3

Responsive design

Make sites work well on phones, tablets, and computers.

4

JavaScript

Add interaction and dynamic behavior to a website.

5

DOM manipulation

Read and change page elements with JavaScript.

6

Web fundamentals

Understand how websites are built, hosted, and published.

Why IMAN Bootcamp?

You learn by doing, with people who support you.

Practical learning

Real projects

Build websites you can show to others.

Mentorship

Get guidance and feedback while you build.

Community

Hands-on practice

Write code in every session instead of only watching.
